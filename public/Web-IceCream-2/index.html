Our story

From one red flower to twelve stores

A tiny cart, a family recipe and a lot of early mornings. Here is how Drop Cream grew up.

2015

The red-flower cart

One cart, one flavour. A strawberry-and-rose recipe that sold out by 6 PM, every single day.

2018

Our first parlour

A 12-seat shop with a pink neon sign. We added cones, cups and the now-famous vanilla rose.

2021

Frozen delivery

Insulated boxes and a promise: it arrives cold or we send another. Orders tripled in a year.

2023

Twelve stores

Twelve parlours, a central kitchen and 80+ people who love dessert as much as we do.

Today

25+ flavours and counting

A flavour lab for seasonal batches and a loyal community of 12,000+ happy customers.

How we make it

From farm to cone in four steps

01

Source

Milk from nearby dairies and fruit picked in season. We know every farmer by name.

02

Churn

Small batches every morning, slowly, so every scoop is creamy and never icy.

03

Taste

Every batch is tasted by our flavour team and only approved when it is perfect.

04

Serve

Scooped fresh in the parlour, or delivered frozen in insulated boxes.

What we stand for

Sweet on the outside, honest inside

Fresh milk, every day

Collected each morning and churned within hours. No powders, no long-life shortcuts.

0 of milk a week

Real fruit

No artificial colours

Vegetarian & eggless

Kinder packaging
