Our story

Coffee is better when it's shared.

It started with one grinder, one oven and a belief: a good cup deserves good company.

We roast in small batches, bake every morning, keep the lights low and the music softer. Whether you're here for a ten-minute espresso or a three-hour catch-up, there's a chair for you.
