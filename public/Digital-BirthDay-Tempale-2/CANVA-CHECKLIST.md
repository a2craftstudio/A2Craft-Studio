# Canva checklist for BirthDay-Tempale-2

Make **one Canva design per theme** and send me its link. Put each picture on its own page, or several per page, and keep them roughly in the order below. Use your own photos or Canva free elements (see the note at the end).

Shapes: **portrait** = taller than wide (about 4:5), **square** = 1:1, **film** = wider than tall (5:4), **print** = any.
