What we collect

  • Orders and enquiries: your name, phone number, address and notes, only when you send them to us.
  • On your device: your cart, saved treats, recent orders and preferences are stored in your own browser. We do not see this data.
  • Email: if you join the Sweet Drip club, we store your email to send offers.

How we use it

To prepare and deliver your order, reply to your enquiry, and, if you joined the club, send you offers. We do not sell your data.

Third parties

Orders and enquiries are sent through WhatsApp, which has its own privacy policy. Our map uses OpenStreetMap, and our fonts come from Google Fonts.

Your choices

You can clear your browser storage at any time to remove saved data. To remove your details from our records or unsubscribe, email hello@sweetdrip.in.
