Who we are

A family that lives on dessert

It started with Dada's hand-cranked gola machine outside Juhu beach. Today Sweet Drip blends those old recipes — matka kulfi, rose falooda, kala khatta gola — with brand-new treats like freakshakes and handmade truffles.

  • Recipes passed down three generations
  • 100% vegetarian kitchen, eggless options
  • Zero artificial colours in our kulfi & ice cream

Milestones

How we grew up

1968

The gola cart

Dada's hand-cranked ice shaver and six syrups at Juhu beach.

1985

Dadi's kulfi

Matka kulfi joins the cart and sells out every evening.

2012

First parlour

A tiny 10-seater with kulfi, rose falooda and golas.

2018

Chocolate kitchen

Vikram starts tempering our own chocolate for truffles and bars.

2021

Frozen delivery

Insulated boxes bring golas and kulfi to your doorstep.

2025

Six parlours & counting

Shakes, sundaes, waffles — and still Dada's gola recipe.
